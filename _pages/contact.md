**I ignore and do not respond to unspecific emails and emails that appear to have been generated primarily using an LLM.**


## Prospective Students

If you contact me about an open positions (PhD, postdoc, or internship), please include the following documents in your email:
- your CV **as a PDF** (please do not send links to your CV; I will not click on them);
- your transcript **as a PDF**.
In your email, **explain specifically which aspects of my research interest you and how your background could contribute to the work of the lab**. For example, identify specific project(s) or publication(s) of mine that interest you and explain what you could contribute.

**I will not respond to generic or impersonal emails, e.g., emails that do not include my name or are a clear copy-paste of emails sent to other professors (including emails sent to other Inria faculty), and emails that appear to have been generated primarily using an LLM.**

PS: adding a PS with the full name of the conference where my last paper was published would certainly catch my attention and increase the likelihood of receiving a response.


## Datasets

Most of our research prototypes are already available on GitHub, e.g., [DoubleX](https://github.com/Aurore54F/DoubleX), [HideNoSeek](https://github.com/Aurore54F/HideNoSeek), [JStap](https://github.com/Aurore54F/JStap), [JaSt](https://github.com/Aurore54F/JaSt), [etc](https://aurore54f.github.io/publications/). Other research prototypes can be found [here](https://github.com/Aurore54F?tab=repositories).

Some of the datasets we used in our experiments are directly referenced in the corresponding papers.
For example,
- [geeksonsecurity/js-malicious-dataset](https://github.com/geeksonsecurity/js-malicious-dataset)
- [HynekPetrak/javascript-malware-collection](https://github.com/HynekPetrak/javascript-malware-collection)

If you need access to other datasets for research purposes, feel free to send me a specific email with the following items:
- Your institutional/academic email address;
- The name of the PI or senior researcher supervising the project, together with their web page and email address, and please CC them on your reply;
- A CV or brief description of your research background;
- A short description of the project and how you intend to use the dataset;
- Any relevant publications or other research outputs in this area, if any.

**I will ignore emails that do not contain these information.**
