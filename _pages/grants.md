Since joining Inria in December 2025, I have acquired the following research grants:


* **2026--2030: [ANR JCJC (EXPELLIARMUS)](https://anr.fr/fileadmin/aap/2026/selection/aapg-2026-selection.pdf), Principal Investigator**
- Competitive young researchers grant, funded by the French National Research Agency (ANR).
- This project supports my research on disarming dangerous browser extensions to protect the security and privacy of Web users.
- Duration: 42 months.
- Total funding: (to come...).


* **2026--2029: IdEx Attractivity Package, Principal Investigator**
- Funding awarded to newly recruited researchers through a project-based selection process, within the Initiative of Excellence (IdEx) of Universite Cote d'Azur.
- This project supports my research on detecting dangerous browser extensions.
- Duration: 3 years.
- Total funding: **50,000€**.


Prior to joining Inria, I held a position with substantial institutional research funding, and therefore did not need to apply for external research grants. The grants listed above were obtained shortly after joining Inria, demonstrating my ability to successfully compete for external funding.
